How the score works

The most AI a piece of work can use is: AI wrote it, AI directed it, AI generated the picture, AI generated the sound. Those four weigh 15 each. Generated images and code come next, then the helpers (tracking, masks and depth maps, motion graphics, sound effects, research), and audio cleanup and mastering weigh least. Each use gets a level: a touch, half, mostly or all AI. Points are weight times level; the score is the sum.
